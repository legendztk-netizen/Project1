# Group Size SKUs on Canonical Product Family Pages

The storefront groups Standard Products that share a core construction on one
canonical Catalog Product Family page and presents their size and connection
combinations as exact SKU Variants. Search and support links may preselect a
variant, but only an exact SKU can enter a Quote List, PI, or fulfillment record.
This preserves the full assortment while avoiding hundreds of repetitive thin
pages and keeping SEO, comparison, and maintenance focused on useful family
content.
