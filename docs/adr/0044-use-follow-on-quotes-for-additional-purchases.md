# Use Follow-on Quotes for Additional Purchases

Products, quantities, or specifications requested after Confirmed Order
creation are purchased through a new Follow-on Quote with its own RFQ, PI,
payment, and Order. The originating Order is not amended. When Operations can
send the purchases together, a Consolidated Dispatch coordinates separate
order-owned Shipments rather than merging Orders, commercial records, or
payment allocations.

The original Order is never delayed automatically. Consolidation requires a
customer Combined Shipping Request, Operations confirmation before carrier
handoff, and acceptance of a Follow-on PI that states any revised dispatch plan
and date. Otherwise the original Order continues under its existing schedule.
