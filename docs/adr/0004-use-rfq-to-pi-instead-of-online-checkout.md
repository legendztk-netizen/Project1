# Use RFQ-to-PI Instead of Online Checkout

The storefront will not accept online checkout or payment. Customers build a
customer-facing Quote List, provide destination and purchasing details, and
submit it to create a numbered RFQ shown on the confirmation page and under
`My Quotes`; RFQ submission does not send a confirmation email. The seller validates specifications,
freight, trade terms, lead time, payment milestones, and cancellation and
refund conditions before issuing a Proforma Invoice and payment instructions.
