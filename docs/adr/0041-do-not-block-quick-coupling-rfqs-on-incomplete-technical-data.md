# Do Not Block Quick Coupling RFQs on Incomplete Technical Data

The 57 launch Quick Coupling SKUs retain `Add to Quote` without requiring manual
completion of every technical field. Technical Data Status is independent of
Catalog Publication Status, RFQ Eligibility, and Qualification Status. Shared
attributes may be inherited from a versioned Specification Profile scoped to
the exact family and variant dimensions that affect the value; SKU overrides
record exceptions. Unresolved attributes remain Pending and are omitted from
the storefront rather than copied from a merely similar competitor product.
Sales confirms any specification required for the proposed application before
PI issuance. RFQ eligibility therefore means that the item can enter review,
not that all technical data or production qualification is complete.
