# Make Approved Custom Products Non-cancellable

A customer may cancel a made-to-order product before Production Approval, with
any approved refund calculated under the documented Refund Fee Allocation.
After Production Approval, products that are cut, crimped, marked, or
custom-packed cannot be cancelled or returned for convenience. This restriction
does not remove the customer's remedies for a Nonconforming Product or the
seller's inability to produce the approved specification.
