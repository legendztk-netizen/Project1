# Route RFQs by Customer Type and Merchandise Subtotal

An RFQ must represent a discounted Merchandise Subtotal of at least $100. An
Individual Customer may submit a DDP RFQ through $4,500; a higher-value request
must be resubmitted by a Business Customer. A Business Customer uses DDP terms
through $3,000 and DAP terms above $3,000. Shipping, duties, import taxes, and
other fees do not count toward these thresholds.
