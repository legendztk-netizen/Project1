# Fix Customer Logistics and DDP Import Charges After PI Acceptance

The logistics charge in an accepted PI remains fixed when product quantity,
Delivery Address, shipment plan, and transport method are unchanged. The seller
bears any later increase caused by its own packing or freight estimate and does
not collect a supplemental payment from the customer. Because the charge is a
fixed service price rather than carrier-cost reimbursement, a lower final cost
does not create an automatic refund. The seller records actual cost internally
but must obtain customer agreement and adjust the quote before materially
downgrading the accepted logistics service. A customer-requested change to the
quoted inputs creates a Quote Revision, a new Shipment Packing Estimate, and a
replacement PI. This gives the customer a reliable accepted total while
preserving a controlled path for commercially meaningful changes.

The same fixed-price treatment applies to DDP import-clearance, duty, and
import-tax estimates when the products, quantities, Delivery Address, and
accurate customer-supplied import information are unchanged. The seller bears
overruns and retains savings. A customer data error or customer-requested change
requires a Quote Revision and replacement PI, while US Sales Tax remains a
separate determination.

The DDP commitment is not tied to one logistics provider. If the quoted provider
becomes unavailable after payment, the seller seeks an alternative DDP route at
its own cost and does not unilaterally convert the Order to DAP. If no compliant
DDP route remains possible, the seller offers a full seller-caused refund unless
the customer explicitly accepts a replacement DAP PI.

The website does not create a customer-facing Customs Review status or send
customs-entry and customs-release notifications. A dispatched Order remains
Shipped, with carrier tracking and Support available for customer questions. If
the Shipment ultimately cannot be delivered because of the seller's declaration,
documentation, or product-compliance failure, the customer chooses a no-cost
replacement or a full seller-caused refund.
