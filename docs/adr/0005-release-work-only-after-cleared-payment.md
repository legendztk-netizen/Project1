# Release Work Only After Cleared Payment

Corporate payment through WorldFirst is the primary collection method. A
seller-issued PayPal payment link may be offered for samples, lower-value
Standard Products, or a first transaction. All launch orders require
Prepayment, and no deposit, balance, or credit terms are offered. Production
and shipment are released only for a Confirmed Order after the full PI Price is
Cleared Funds net of bank deductions; customer remittance screenshots are never
accepted as proof of receipt. Bank-transfer customers bear remitting and
intermediary bank charges, while seller-issued PayPal links do not add a fee
after payment.
