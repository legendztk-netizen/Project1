# Returns Policy

## Standard Products

- The customer must request a Return Authorization within 30 calendar days
  after delivery.
- The product must be uninstalled, unused, uncut, complete, and in its original
  packaging.
- A return caused by incorrect selection or a change of mind is shipped to the
  US Return Location at the customer's expense.
- Original cross-border freight, duties, import taxes, and clearance charges
  are not refundable for a convenience return.
- No restocking fee applies when inspection confirms that the returned product
  is complete and resalable.
- A customer-caused refund may deduct only documented, non-refundable
  third-party bank or payment-channel costs where permitted by law and the
  accepted terms. No separate handling fee or markup applies.
- An unsolicited package without a Return Authorization is not accepted.

## Seller Error or Nonconforming Product

When the seller ships the wrong product or delivers a Nonconforming Product,
or when the seller cannot supply the agreed product, the seller is responsible
for the agreed replacement, return, and refund costs. The customer receives the
full approved refund without a deduction for bank or payment-channel fees.

## Made-to-order Products

Hose Assemblies and other made-to-order products are governed by Production
Approval and are not eligible for convenience return after approval. Remedies
remain available for a Nonconforming Product.

## Return Location

Eligible US returns are sent only to the internally documented
[US Return Location](../operations/us-return-location.md). Its address is
provided to the customer only in an approved RA and is not published as a
general return address, customer pickup point, or retail location.
