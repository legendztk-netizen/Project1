# Returns Policy

This is the single launch return policy (Spec 7). There are no pre-launch
Orders, so no earlier return policy is carried forward. Newly issued PIs freeze
this policy's terms (`pi-refund-2026-09-27-v2`); the public summary is served at
`/policies/returns`.

## Standard Products

- The customer may request return review within 14 calendar days after the
  actual delivery date of the applicable Shipment. The delivery date in
  `America/New_York` is day 0; requests close at 11:59 PM ET on day 14. For
  example, delivery on September 1 closes the window at 11:59 PM ET on
  September 15. Split Shipments each use their own delivery date.
- The product must be uninstalled, unused, uncut, complete, and in its original
  packaging.
- A request opens an After-sales Case. It does not authorize a return. Goods are
  accepted only under an issued Return Authorization (RA) and must arrive within
  30 calendar days after RA issuance (issuance ET date is day 0).
- A return caused by incorrect selection or a change of mind is shipped at the
  customer's expense.
- An approved convenience return carries a 10% restocking fee on the discounted
  merchandise amount approved for return.
- Performed outbound DDP shipping, duties, import taxes, and clearance charges
  are not refunded for a convenience return. Applicable Sales Tax is adjusted
  separately under the accepted tax treatment.
- The same terms apply when inspection shows that a reported problem was
  caused by the buyer, for example incorrect selection, installation damage
  or misuse. Admin records that responsibility in the inspection decision with
  a customer-visible reason. Orders whose PI accepted earlier refund terms
  (`pi-refund-2026-09-27-v1`) keep seller terms for every problem report.
- A customer-caused refund may deduct only documented, non-refundable
  third-party bank or payment-channel costs where permitted by law and the
  accepted terms, shown gross-to-net. No separate handling fee or markup
  applies.
- An unsolicited package without a Return Authorization is not accepted.

## Seller Error or Nonconforming Product

When the seller ships the wrong product, the product is damaged in transit, the
seller delivers a Nonconforming Product, or the seller cannot supply the agreed
product, the seller is responsible for the agreed replacement, return, and
refund costs, including reasonable logistics and the Cutting and Labeling Fee
where relevant. No restocking fee applies, and the customer receives the full
approved refund without a deduction for bank or payment-channel fees. These
reports are not limited by the 14-day convenience-return window.

## Made-to-order Products

Hose Assemblies, cut-length hose, and other made-to-order products are governed
by Production Approval and are not eligible for convenience return after
approval or cutting. Remedies remain available for seller error, damage, or a
Nonconforming Product.

## Inspection and Refunds

- Returned goods are inspected after receipt. Operations targets a decision
  (`Approved`, `Partially Approved`, or `Declined`) within 5 US business days
  after receipt. Every decision includes a reason.
- An approved refund is initiated within 10 US business days after approval
  through the original payment channel where possible, to an account verified
  for the same Purchasing Context. The website records the initiation; it does
  not promise the bank or PayPal posting date. There are no cash or store-credit
  refunds.

## Return Location

Eligible US returns are sent only to the Return Location selected in an issued
RA. Its address is provided to the customer only in that RA and is not
published as a general return address, customer pickup point, or retail
location.
