# Conflicts and Assumptions Register

## Status Vocabulary

- `Resolved`: a later explicit user decision or identified source settles it.
- `Accepted`: an architectural assumption is explicitly approved as the
  implementation baseline.
- `Pending confirmation`: the project must not silently choose one side.
- `Proposed`: a bounded implementation choice introduced by the PRD/Spec and
  awaiting explicit acceptance.

those sources.

## Explicitly Confirmed, Not Assumptions

The following must not be reopened as synthesis assumptions: guests may browse,
configure, and build an Anonymous Quote List; unfinished guest drafts are not
recoverable after leaving; email verification is required for cross-session
save, RFQ submission, and Personal Center; compatibility may be RFQ-Eligible
without being production-qualified; and no payment or checkout occurs on the
website. Issue #2 remains the only `ready-for-agent` Delivery Spec while Issues
#3-#9 are marked `blocked` by their explicit prerequisites.
